TITLE
Zapp Pay Merchant

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Ku aqbal lacag bixinta QR, daawo iibkaaga oo ka bixi dakhliga Zapp Pay.

FULL DESCRIPTION
Zapp Pay Merchant waa app-ka dukaamada, ganacsiyada iyo wakiilada ku qaata lacagaha Zapp Pay.

KU AQBAL LACAGAHA
• Muuji koodhkaaga QR si macaamiisha ugu bixiyaan iyagoo iskaanaynaya
• Geli lacag si aad u sameyso codsi lacag bixin
• Isticmaal koodhka kaaliyaha marka macmiilku aanu iskaan gareyn karin
• Arag lacag bixin kasta isla markii la bixiyo

U FAHAN GANACSIGAAGA
• Haraagaaga iyo iibka maanta hal shaashad
• Taariikh buuxda oo lacag bixinno, dib-celin iyo ka-bixin ah
• Warbixinno aad wadaagi karto

KA BIXI DAKHLIGAAGA
• U dir haraagaaga lambarkaaga Zaad ama eDahab
• Si isku mar ah u bixi dad badan adigoo isticmaalaya mushaharka

SHAQAALEHAAGA KU GAAR
• Shaqaalaha sii marin u gaar ah: sarrifle, waiter ama maamule
• Shaqaaluhu waxay aqbali karaan lacag iyagoon arkin ganacsigaaga oo dhan

WAKIILADA
• Macaamiisha ku shubo oo u bixi lacag caddaan ah
• La soco float-kaaga iyo komishankaaga

AMAAN LAGA BILAABAY
• Lacag bixin kasta waxaa ilaaliya PIN-kaaga
• Ku gal lambarkaaga taleefanka iyo koodh hal mar la isticmaalo
• Waxaa ku jira Ingiriis, Soomaali iyo Carabi

Ku gal lambarkaaga taleefanka. Ganacsiyada iyo wakiilada cusub waxay ka codsadaan app-ka gudihiisa, kooxda Zapp Pay ayaa ansixisa.

Caawimaad ma u baahan tahay? Booqo https://zapppay.web.app/support
