TITLE
Zapp Pay Merchant

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Accept QR payments, see your sales and pay out your earnings with Zapp Pay.

FULL DESCRIPTION
Zapp Pay Merchant is the app for shops, businesses and agents who take payments with Zapp Pay.

ACCEPT PAYMENTS
• Show your QR code and let customers scan it to pay
• Enter an amount to create a payment request
• Use a counter code when a customer cannot scan
• See each payment arrive the moment it is made

KNOW YOUR BUSINESS
• Your balance and today's sales on one screen
• A full history of payments, refunds and payouts
• Statements you can share

PAY OUT YOUR EARNINGS
• Send your balance to your Zaad or eDahab number
• Pay several people at once with payroll

RUN IT WITH YOUR TEAM
• Give staff their own access as cashier, waiter or manager
• Staff accept payments without seeing your whole business

FOR AGENTS
• Top up customers and cash them out
• Track your float and your commission

SAFE BY DESIGN
• Every payment is protected by your PIN
• Sign in with your phone number and a one-time code
• Available in English, Somali and Arabic

Sign in with your phone number. New businesses and agents apply inside the app and are approved by the Zapp Pay team.

Need help? Visit https://zapppay.web.app/support
