1. Who we are

Zapp Pay is a mobile payments service operating in Somaliland. "Zapp Pay", "we", "us" and "our" refer to the operator of the Zapp Pay and Zapp Pay Merchant applications. Zapp Pay is currently an unregistered trading name; you can reach the operator at the email address in section 15.

We are responsible for the personal information described in this policy. By creating a Zapp Pay account you agree to the handling of your information as set out here.

3. Information we collect

a. Information you give us

  • Phone number — used as your account identity and verified by SMS one-time code.
  • Your name, and optionally an email address, date of birth, gender and city or district.
  • A 6-digit PIN, which you set to authorise payments. We store only a hash of it.
  • Your preferred language (English, Somali or Arabic).
  • Mobile money numbers you link, such as your Zaad or eDahab number.
  • Identity documents, if you choose to verify your identity — the type and number of your national ID, passport or driver's licence, photographs of that document, and a selfie. You only provide these if you request a verified account tier.
  • Business details, for merchants and agents — business name, trading area, opening hours and the services you offer.

b. Information created when you use Zapp Pay

  • Transaction records — amounts, dates, counterparties, references and status for payments, QR payments, top-ups, cash-outs and transfers.
  • Wallet and ledger entries recording balances and the double-entry postings behind each transaction.
  • Your QR codes and the payment requests generated from them.
  • Referral information — your referral code, who referred you, and how many people you have referred.
  • Security events — sign-in times, failed PIN attempts and temporary lockouts, which we use to detect fraud and protect your balance.

c. Information from your device

  • A push notification token, so we can alert you about payments. You can turn notifications off in your device settings.
  • Crash and diagnostic reports collected by Firebase Crashlytics when the app stops unexpectedly. These contain device model, operating system version and a technical stack trace.
  • Device integrity signals collected by Firebase App Check, which confirm requests come from a genuine copy of our app rather than an attacker's script.

4. What we don't collect

  • We do not collect your card details. When you fund a wallet by card, the card form is served by Stripe and the card number, expiry and security code go directly to Stripe. We receive only a payment reference and the last four digits.
  • We do not collect your precise location. The app does not request location permission.
  • We do not read your SMS messages or call logs. The apps do not request those permissions.
  • We do not collect advertising identifiers and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not receive your fingerprint or face data. See section 6.

6. Camera, photos and Face ID

Camera

The app asks for camera access so you can scan payment QR codes and, if you verify your identity, photograph your ID document. Scanning happens on your device. We do not record video, and camera images are never uploaded unless you are deliberately submitting an identity document.

Photo library

The app asks for photo library access so you can save your own QR code as an image. We do not read or upload your other photos.

Face ID and fingerprint

If you enable biometric unlock, your device performs the check locally and tells the app only whether it succeeded. Your fingerprint and face data stay on your device and are never transmitted to us. We never receive or store biometric data.

7. Who we share information with

We do not sell your information. We share it only with the service providers below, only to the extent they need it to run the service on our behalf, and only under contracts that require them to protect it.

We will also disclose information where we are legally required to, specifically:

  • in response to a valid order from a court or competent authority;
  • to investigate suspected fraud or to protect the rights, property or safety of our users or the public;
  • to a merchant or agent involved in a transaction, limited to what they need to identify that transaction — a counterparty sees your name and the payment details, never your ID documents, contact details or balance.

If Zapp Pay is ever acquired or merged, your information may transfer to the new operator. We will tell you before that happens and this policy will continue to apply until you are given notice of a replacement.

8. Where your information is stored

Zapp Pay runs on Google Cloud infrastructure, and your information is stored and processed on servers located in the United States. This means your information leaves Somaliland. We rely on Google's contractual and technical safeguards to protect it in transit and at rest. By using Zapp Pay you consent to this transfer.

9. How long we keep it

  • Account information — for as long as your account is open.
  • Transaction and ledger records — retained after account closure where we need them for financial record-keeping, dispute resolution or legal obligations. These records are kept in a form that no longer identifies you where that is possible.
  • Identity documents — kept while your account is open and deleted with your account, unless a legal obligation requires us to keep them longer.
  • Crash reports — retained by Firebase Crashlytics on its standard schedule, currently 90 days.

10. Deleting your account

In the app

Open Profile → About → Delete Account, confirm, and enter your PIN.

Two things to know before you delete:

  • Your wallet balance must be zero. Withdraw or spend any remaining balance first — we block deletion while money is still in the account so that funds are not stranded.
  • Deletion is not instant. Your account is deactivated straight away and can no longer be used, and your personal information is permanently deleted within 30 days. Transaction records are retained as described in section 9.

If you cannot access the app, email us from the address on your account, or from any address together with the phone number on the account, and we will process the deletion for you.

11. Your choices

  • See and correct your information — most of it is editable in your profile. For anything else, ask us.
  • Get a copy of your information — email us and we will send you an export.
  • Delete your account — see section 10.
  • Turn off notifications — in the app's notification settings, or in your device settings.
  • Complain — write to us first. We will investigate and reply.

We will not restrict your use of Zapp Pay because you exercised any of these choices.

12. How we protect your information

  • All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit.
  • Your PIN is stored as a hash, never in a readable form, and repeated wrong guesses temporarily lock PIN entry.
  • Database access is governed by server-side security rules, so one user cannot read another user's records.
  • Requests are validated with Firebase App Check to block traffic that does not come from a genuine copy of the app.
  • Balances are maintained in a double-entry ledger with automated integrity checks, so transactions cannot silently go missing.

No system is perfectly secure. Keep your PIN to yourself — Zapp Pay staff will never ask you for it, and anyone who does is attempting fraud. If you think your account has been accessed by someone else, contact us immediately.

13. Children

Zapp Pay is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.
