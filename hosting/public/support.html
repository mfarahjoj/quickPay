Support

Help with the Zapp Pay app and the Zapp Pay Merchant app. Email us any time — or check the answers below first, most questions are covered there.

Email us

support@zapppay.so

We reply within 2 business days, usually sooner. We answer in English and Somali.

To get a faster answer, include

  • The phone number on your Zapp Pay account.
  • Which app you are using — Zapp Pay or Zapp Pay Merchant.
  • Your phone model and whether it is iPhone or Android.
  • For a payment problem: the date, the amount, and the transaction reference from your history screen.
  • A screenshot, if something looks wrong on screen.

Never send us your PIN. Zapp Pay staff will never ask for it, by email, phone or message. Anyone asking for your PIN is trying to steal from you.

Common questions

I didn't get my verification code

The code arrives by SMS within about a minute. If it doesn't:

  • Check that the phone number you entered is correct, including the country code.
  • Make sure you have network coverage — SMS will not arrive on Wi-Fi alone.
  • Wait for the timer to finish, then tap Resend.
  • If you have requested several codes quickly, wait a few minutes before trying again.

Still nothing after two attempts? Email us with the phone number you are trying to register.

I forgot my PIN, or my PIN is locked

Your PIN is 6 digits and we cannot look it up — it is stored in a form nobody can read, including us. You can reset it from the sign-in screen by verifying your phone number again.

If you entered the wrong PIN several times in a row, PIN entry locks temporarily to protect your balance. Wait for the lock to expire and try again, or reset your PIN.

I paid a merchant but the payment isn't showing

Check your history screen first — if the payment appears there, it went through.

If it doesn't appear:

  • Confirm the money actually left your mobile money account by checking the SMS from your provider.
  • Ask the merchant to check their Zapp Pay Merchant app for a pending entry.
  • Pull down to refresh your history screen.

If your provider confirms the money left but neither app shows it, email us the date, amount, merchant name and the provider's SMS reference, and we will trace it.

The QR code won't scan
  • Make sure Zapp Pay has camera permission — check your phone's settings for the app.
  • Hold the phone steady about 15–20 cm from the code, with the code inside the frame.
  • Poor light or glare on a printed code is the usual cause. Try shading it, or ask the merchant to show the code on their screen instead.
  • If the code is damaged or worn, the merchant can print a fresh one from their app.
How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Profile → About → Delete Account and confirm with your PIN.

Two things to know:

  • Your balance must be zero first — withdraw or spend anything remaining, otherwise the funds would be stranded.
  • Your account is deactivated immediately and your personal information is permanently deleted within 30 days. Transaction records are kept where we are required to.

Can't get into the app? Email us from the address on your account, or include the phone number registered to it, and we will do it for you. See the privacy policy for the full detail.

I need to change the phone number on my account

Your phone number is your account identity, so it cannot be changed from inside the app. Email us from your registered email address, or with enough detail to confirm the account is yours, and tell us the old and new numbers. We will verify your identity before making the change.

I think someone else used my account

Email us immediately at support@zapppay.so with the word "URGENT" in the subject line.

In the meantime:

  • Change your PIN from the app.
  • Write down any transactions you don't recognise — date, amount and reference.
  • If your phone was lost or stolen, tell your mobile provider so they can block the SIM. That prevents anyone receiving your verification codes.
I'm a merchant — how do I get set up?

Download Zapp Pay Merchant, register with your business phone number and complete your business profile. New merchant accounts are reviewed before they can accept payments, and we will contact you once yours is approved.

Email us if you want to be onboarded in person — we visit merchants in Hargeisa.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Email us with what you expected to happen, what happened instead, and a screenshot if you have one. Bug reports from users are genuinely how we find most problems, and we read every one.

Your data

Our privacy policy explains everything we collect, who we share it with, where it is stored and how to have it deleted. Short version: we don't sell your data, we don't track you, and we can't see your PIN or your card number.
