For developers

Take Zapp Pay online with one API

Create a charge from your server; your customer approves it in the Zapp Pay app with their PIN; you get a webhook when it's paid. You never touch their credentials, and nothing moves until they approve.

  • Hosted checkout page — or build your own flow
  • Idempotent charges and refunds, so retries never double-charge
  • Signed webhooks for paid, expired and refunded events
  • Full test mode — develop without moving real money
  • Create API keys yourself in the Merchant app, under Settings → Developers
# Charge a customer $12.50
curl https://zapppay.web.app/v1/charges \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $ZAPP_KEY" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: order-1001-a1" \
  -d '{
    "amount": 1250,
    "reference": "Order #1001",
    "success_url": "https://shop.example/thanks"
  }'

# → 201 { "id": "ch_…", "status": "pending",
#         "checkout_url": "https://…/checkout/ch_…" }

Security

Built like a ledger, not an app

Zapp Pay is engineered the way banks keep books: every movement of money is a balanced, immutable double-entry record. Nothing is ever edited or deleted — so nothing can silently go missing.

🔐

Your PIN approves every payment

No money leaves your wallet without your 6-digit PIN. It's stored only as a cryptographic hash — nobody at Zapp Pay can read it — and repeated wrong guesses lock PIN entry to protect your balance.

📒

Double-entry, always balanced

Every transaction posts matching ledger entries that must sum to zero, with automated integrity checks running against them. Your balance is provable, not just a number on a screen.

📵

Only genuine apps get through

Requests are verified with device integrity checks, so traffic that doesn't come from a real copy of the app is rejected before it reaches your account.

🕶️

Private by default

We don't sell data, don't run ads, and don't track you across other apps. Your PIN and balance stay private to you. Read the full privacy policy.
