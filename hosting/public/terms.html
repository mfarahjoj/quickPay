3. Your account and PIN

Your 6-digit PIN authorises payments from your wallet. It is the key to your money, and the responsibilities around it are yours:

  • Keep your PIN secret. Never share it with anyone — including people claiming to work for Zapp Pay. Our staff will never ask for it.
  • Anything approved with your PIN counts as approved by you. If you share your PIN or let someone use your unlocked phone, payments they make are treated as yours.
  • Tell us immediately if you believe someone else knows your PIN or has used your account. Change your PIN in the app and contact support.
  • Repeated wrong PIN entries temporarily lock PIN entry. After you reset your PIN, outgoing payments may be paused for a security period (with a small spending allowance so you are not stranded). This protects you if someone takes over your phone number.

Your phone number receives your verification codes. If your phone or SIM is lost or stolen, tell your mobile provider to block the SIM, then contact us.

4. Your wallet

  • Your wallet holds US dollars. Balances are stored value in our ledger — your wallet is not a bank account, it earns no interest, and it is not covered by any deposit insurance scheme.
  • Every movement of money is recorded as a balanced double-entry ledger record. Your balance in the app reflects those records.
  • If an obvious error credits your wallet with money that is not yours — a duplicate, a mistake, a technical fault — we may reverse it with a correcting entry. We will tell you when this happens.
  • You can spend, send or cash out your balance at any time the service is available, subject to the limits in section 8.

5. Payments and transfers

  • Check before you confirm. The app shows the recipient and the amount before you enter your PIN. Once you approve a payment, it settles immediately.

  • Merchant payment requests expire quickly — if you don't approve in time, the request lapses and nothing is charged. Ask the merchant to raise it again.
  • Refunds for purchases come from the merchant. If you have a problem with goods or services you paid for, resolve it with the merchant — they can refund you in full or in part through their app. Zapp Pay moves the money; the sale itself is between you and the merchant.

7. Top-ups and agents

  • You can load your wallet by handing cash to an authorised Zapp Pay agent. Confirm the amount shown in your app before you leave — the top-up appears in your balance immediately.
  • Agents are independent businesses authorised to process top-ups. The cash transaction at the counter is between you and the agent; the wallet credit is performed by our system and backed by float the agent has pre-purchased.
  • Never hand cash to anyone who cannot show a confirmed top-up in your app. If the balance does not appear on the spot, do not leave the cash.

8. Identity verification and limits

  • We may ask you to verify or re-verify your identity before processing a transaction, and we may hold or decline transactions until verification is complete.
